Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5th August 2026

Safety Within Coaching is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how I collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit this website, book a session, or sign up to receive updates from me.

1. Who I Am

For the purposes of data protection law, I, Kati Kumpulainen, trading as Safety Within Coaching, am the data controller responsible for your personal information. You can reach me at safetywithincoaching@gmail.com.

2. What I Collect

I may collect the following personal information:

  • Your name and email address (when you contact me, book a session, or subscribe to updates)
  • Any other information you voluntarily share via forms, email, or during coaching sessions, such as details about what you'd like support with
  • Basic technical data collected automatically by this website (such as general usage statistics), if analytics tools are used

3. Why I Collect It

I collect your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and arrange coaching sessions
  • Provide coaching services to you as a client
  • Send occasional updates about my services, offers, and news from Safety Within Coaching, where you've agreed to receive these

I will not use your information for any other purpose without telling you and, where required, asking for your consent.

4. My Legal Basis for Processing Your Information

Under data protection law, I rely on the following legal grounds:

  • Contract: processing your name, email, and session details is necessary to arrange and deliver the coaching you've booked
  • Consent: for sending marketing emails or newsletter updates, which you can withdraw at any time
  • Legitimate interest: for basic record-keeping and responding to enquiries, in a way that doesn't override your rights

5. How Long I Keep Your Information

I keep client information for as long as needed to provide coaching services and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes, generally no longer than a few years unless a longer period is required by law. Newsletter contact details are kept until you unsubscribe. If you'd like your information deleted sooner, you're welcome to ask.

6. How I Store and Protect Your Information

Your information is stored using trusted third-party platforms that implement their own security measures to protect your data. I do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to others.

7. Your Rights

Under data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information I hold about you
  • Ask me to correct or update inaccurate information
  • Ask me to delete your information, where applicable
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Unsubscribe from any email at any time using the link at the bottom of the message

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at safetywithincoaching@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto), Finland's supervisory authority for data protection.

8. Third-Party Services and International Transfers

I use the following trusted third-party platforms to run this website and deliver my services:

  • GitHub — stores the website's code
  • Vercel — hosts and serves this website to visitors
  • Calendly — manages call bookings and scheduling
  • Formspree — delivers contact form submissions to my email

These providers are based outside Finland and the EU/EEA. Where that's the case, they are required to provide appropriate safeguards for your data, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent adequacy mechanism, in accordance with their own privacy policies. I encourage you to review those policies directly if you'd like more detail on how each handles data.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most recent version will always be available on this website.
